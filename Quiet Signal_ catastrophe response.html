    How it decides

    • Need score = damage (satellite and drone imagery) + age 75+, medical equipment, no car, thin cash cushion.
    • Waiting time is added to the score, so nobody gets starved. Everyone is reached by the 72nd hour.
    • Vulnerability only changes who is helped first. It never changes coverage or claim decisions.
    • People make every decision. The tool explains each ranking in plain words and can be audited.
    • Synthetic data only. Real welfare checks need consent and local responder coordination.
